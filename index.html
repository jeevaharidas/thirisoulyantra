Frequently Asked Questions

Do you predict the future?

No. I do not read fixed futures or guaranteed outcomes; instead, readings analyze present energies, patterns, and potential paths based on your current state and choices.

What kind of questions should I ask?

Questions should be focused on your specific energy, decisions, challenges, and personal growth to provide deeper and clearer guidance.

Are my readings and personal details confidential?

Yes, all personal information and shared details are handled with strict confidentiality, and I am the only person conducting and accessing your reading.

Can I ask questions about another person?

This is only permitted where energetic access or permission is present, as I reserve the right to decline questions that violate ethical or spiritual boundaries.

How do payments and refunds work?

All payments are fixed and non-refundable, as the reading process begins the moment payment is received.
